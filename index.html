Penguin Software Adventures

Two illustrated Apple II adventures from the early eighties, ported to the browser. The original artwork is not bitmaps — it is vector programs re-run through a 6502 emulator and the real Applesoft ROM, exactly as the machine drew it.

Transylvania

1982 · Antonio Antiochia

Find the vanished Princess Sabrina before sunrise. A werewolf stalks the forest, and something worse waits in the castle.

Play

The Quest

1983 · Dallas Snell

Gorn is sent to slay the dragon laying waste to the kingdom. Cross the desert, the mountains and the sea — and pick your companions well.
