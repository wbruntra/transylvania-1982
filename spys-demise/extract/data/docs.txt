SPY'S DEMISE
                 BY
             ALAN ZELDIN

   (TRANSLATION BY WAYNE BARBAREK)



  LOADSTAR HAS SOMETHING SPECIAL FOR

YOU THIS MONTH -- A "BLUE PLATE

SPECIAL."  AND JUST WHAT IS A "BLUE

PLATE SPECIAL"?  FROM TIME TO TIME,

LOADSTAR WILL DEVOTE AN ENTIRE SIDE

OF A DISK TO A PROGRAM OF EXCEPTIONAL

QUALITY AND INTEREST -- A "BLUE PLATE

SPECIAL."



  ON SIDE ONE OF LOADSTAR'S BONUS

DISK, YOU WILL FIND THIS MONTH'S "BLUE

PLATE SPECIAL," SPY'S DEMISE, A

COMMERCIAL ARCADER FROM PENGUIN-

/POLARWARE.



  IN SPY'S DEMISE YOU ARE AN INTREPID

SECRET AGENT.  YOUR MISSION --

COLLECT THE PIECES OF A CRYPTOGRAM

THAT HOLDS THE KEY TO VALUABLE

COMPUTER DATA.  HERE'S THE CATCH...

EACH PIECE OF THE ENCODED MESSAGE LIES

ON A DIFFERENT FLOOR OF A HOTEL, A

HOTEL UNDER THE CAREFUL GUARD OF A

GROUP OF RATHER NASTY KGB AGENTS.



  YOU MUST MANEUVER YOUR WAY THROUGH

EACH FLOOR, PICKING UP THE MICROFILM,

SECRET DECODER RINGS, AND OTHER TOOLS

OF THE SPY TRADE WHILE AVOIDING THE

KGB AGENTS WHO PATROL IN ELEVATORS.

WHEN (AND IF) YOU MAKE IT TO THE TOP

OF A FLOOR, YOU WILL FIND A PIECE OF

THE CRYPTOGRAM.  ULTIMATELY, WHEN YOU

HAVE ALL THE PIECES, YOU SHOULD BE

ABLE TO LEARN THE SECRET.




PLAYING THE GAME
----------------



  WHEN YOU RUN SPY'S DEMISE, YOU

SHOULD FIRST COME TO A TITLE SCREEN.

YOU MUST TELL THE PROGRAM WHETHER

YOU'RE USING A (J)OYSTICK, (K)EYBOARD,

OR (P)ADDLES.  (NOTE: IF THE DEMO IS

RUNNING, JUST PRESS ANY KEY TO RETURN

TO THE TITLE SCREEN.)


  YOU THEN GO TO THE PLAYING SCREEN.

THE POSSIBLE MOVEMENTS ARE EASY TO

REMEMBER: LEFT AND RIGHT. (IF YOU ARE

PLAYING IN KEYBOARD MODE, THE UP/DOWN

CURSOR KEY MOVES YOU LEFT; THE

LEFT/RIGHT CURSOR KEY MOVES YOU

RIGHT.)


  ON THE RIGHT HAND SIDE OF THE PLAY-

ING SCREEN, ALONG WITH YOUR SCORE, THE

HIGH SCORE, AND THE NUMBER OF LIVES

YOU HAVE LEFT, IS A TIMER.  THE

SOONER YOU COMPLETE A LEVEL, THE MORE

POINTS YOU RECEIVE.  PICKING UP A

SECRET DECODER RING IS WORTH BONUS

POINTS.


  AFTER YOU HAVE MADE IT TO THE TOP OF

A FLOOR AND HAVE RECORDED THE PIECE

OF THE CRYPTOGRAM YOU FOUND THERE,

PRESS A KEY TO MOVE TO ANOTHER BUILD-

ING.


  NOTICE THAT THIS BUILDING IS

SLIGHTLY SHORTER THAN THE PREVIOUS

ONE.  THE NUMBER OF GUARDS REMAINS THE

SAME, THOUGH, MAKING THE NEW BUILDING

TOUGHER TO NEGOTIATE.



  OK, YOU'VE BEEN THOROUGHLY BRIEFED.

YOU KNOW YOUR MISSION -- GET GOING.

AND GOOD LUCK... YOU'RE GOING TO NEED

IT!
